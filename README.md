A Stock Portfolio Tracker is a financial management tool designed to help investors efficiently monitor and manage their stock market investments. It provides a centralized platform where users can add stocks, record purchase and selling transactions, track current market prices, and calculate overall profit or loss. The tracker can display important information such as the total investment amount, current portfolio value, individual stock performance, percentage returns, and portfolio allocation. It may also provide charts and graphs to visualize investment growth and help users understand how their portfolio is performing over time. Advanced portfolio trackers can include features such as dividend tracking, price alerts, historical performance analysis, and real-time market updates. By organizing investment information in a clear and accessible way, a stock portfolio tracker helps users keep track of their financial assets and make data-based investment decisions.
